Mobile redesign — one screen per section

Six sections, each sized to a single phone screen with a clean bottom edge — no section bleeds into the next. Shared header system, one 20px edge rhythm, one horizontal swipe idiom used the same way everywhere.

One full screen per section Space Mono kicker + Poppins title 20px edge rhythm Peek-carousel = the one swipe idiom Dot-matrix lives behind the hero only 44px+ touch targets
01 · HOME

Lead with the crate

Change: name drops to a letterhead line; “Put a record on.” + a loud search field are the first thing you see. The deck sits ready but quiet below until you drop a track on it.

02 · ABOUT

Fills its own screen

Change: full-width portrait, left-aligned bio, all six fact chips in one tidy wrapped row. Sized to the screen instead of floating in a half-empty void.

03 · EXPERIENCE

Swipe photo cards

Reworked: a photo card at a natural landscape crop (no stretch), year on the scrim, then role + description anchored at the bottom of the screen. Swipe with hard snap; next card peeks. Drops the thin filmstrip and the centre-focus scale.

04 · MY TASTE

Same poster, one screen

Keeps: torn cards, tape, tilt, Anton type. Change: voids between zones close up, one artist swipe row, the top 5 becomes a numbered list in the crate card. All fits one screen.

05 · PROJECTS

Kept — just fitted

Keeps: liner-note tracklist, mono index numeral in its pressing hue, role kicker, 2-line preview. Change: rows breathe to fill exactly one screen.

06 · CONNECT

Trimmed down

Change: cassette chrome dropped to a single spine stripe + two reel dots, 44px inputs, full-width send. Footer socials grow; the footer sits at the bottom of the screen where it belongs.

Dark theme shown — the light “Studio Paper” theme inherits every layout change.
Separate from the mockups: on load the page can restore an old scroll position and land mid-hero — a small code fix (disable scroll restoration, pin to top), not a design change.
